Privacy Policy for Raven Rise

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Raven Rise ("the app") is published by Whitemamba Studios. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. It applies to the Android version of Raven Rise distributed through Google Play.

Information the app itself collects

The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

Your progress — high score, coins, unlocked items and settings — is stored only on your own device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address (not GPS);
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system version and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped;
  • diagnostic and crash information relating to ad delivery.

This data is collected by Google as an independent party and is governed by Google's own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the AdMob privacy information.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent interface. You may decline, and you may change your answer later from the privacy option in the app where available. Declining does not stop you using the app; you may simply see less relevant ads.

On any device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads, which limits how ads are personalised.

Children

Raven Rise is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the address below and it will be removed.

Data sharing and retention

No data is sold. Beyond the advertising data described above, which is handled by Google, no information is shared with third parties. Because the app keeps your progress only on your device, uninstalling the app removes it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised, and material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
